# Davel Trace mobile

## Direction

A Đà Nẵng field guide. Warm paper surfaces and pine ink support quick reading. Ochre marks estimates and warnings. A small recurring route diagram identifies the product on entry screens. It is decorative and never carries geographic information.

## System

- Product screens use Be Vietnam Pro, aligned content edges and flat surfaces. Reserve the most prominent type for screen names, trip names and the budget.
- Use pine for a screen's primary action, full borders for neutral controls, and subdued fills for secondary actions. Data sources and warnings retain clear text labels.
- Keep the four labeled navigation destinations. Keep persistent actions in easy reach and settings, archive and destructive actions in their existing menus.
- Preserve logical text scaling, form scrolling with the keyboard and labeled controls. Verify layout at 320 and 390 logical pixels. Test authentication and planner/map workflows through the existing fixtures.
