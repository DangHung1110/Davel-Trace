# Davel Trace mobile

The interface takes its colors from the coast of Đà Nẵng. Ocean blue anchors
actions, deep navy carries text, and a cool off-white background keeps itinerary
and expense rows readable outdoors. Use Be Vietnam Pro throughout. A photo of
the Dragon Bridge belongs at the entrance to the app, where it orients travelers
to the supported destination. The map and real trip details carry the visual
weight during a trip.

The trip list offers direct entry to group trips alongside personal trips. Each
group has a single workspace for the itinerary, suggestions, people, chat, and
money. Within each screen, order content by the traveler's next decision. Keep a
visible distinction between suggestions, proposals under discussion, and the
confirmed schedule. Show the origin of an estimated route in plain language.

Use solid blue for the primary action, lightly tinted surfaces for selection,
and a divider for adjacent rows. Put a visible label on actions whose meaning
cannot be inferred from the icon. Reserve amber for estimates or checks and red
for errors or destructive confirmations. Leave room for keyboard insets and
enlarged type. Test compact layouts at 320 and 390 logical pixels.
